# Grievance Redressal

Under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can raise a complaint about content, another user, a moderation decision, or how we handle your personal data.

## How to complain
Email the Grievance Officer with:
- your registered mobile number (or the last 4 digits) and your name in the app;
- what happened, when, and who was involved (their name in the app, and the date and time of the call if relevant);
- what you would like us to do.

You can also report a user directly from a call or from **Call details** in the app.

## What happens next
- We **acknowledge** your complaint within **24 hours**.
- We **resolve** it within **15 days** and tell you the outcome.
- Complaints about content showing nudity or sexual acts of a person, or impersonation, are acted on within **24 hours**.

If you are not satisfied with our response about your personal data, you can approach the **Data Protection Board of India**.
