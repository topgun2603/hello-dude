# Community Guidelines

{{APP_NAME}} is for kind, respectful conversations in your own language. These rules apply to everyone — callers and companions — on voice and video.

## Be kind
- Treat everyone with respect. Disagree politely.
- Anyone can end a call at any time. No one owes anyone their time.

## Never allowed
- **Anyone under 18.** If someone seems underage, report them.
- **Nudity or sexual content** — on video, in words, or in requests. Video is checked for nudity on your phone; flagged calls are blurred and reviewed.
- **Harassment, bullying, threats, or hate** based on religion, caste, gender, sexuality, language, region or anything else.
- **Asking for or sharing contact details** — phone numbers, WhatsApp, Instagram, addresses, or UPI IDs.
- **Money outside the app** — asking for payments, recharges, loans or gifts outside {{APP_NAME}}; scams of any kind.
- **Meeting in person** or arranging relationships, escort or adult services.
- **Other people's private information**, or recording/screenshotting calls without permission.
- **Pretending to be someone else**, or using another person's account or documents.
- **Violence, self-harm encouragement, illegal drugs or anything illegal.**

## Report and block
Tap **Report** during or after a call. Reporting also blocks that person from reaching you again. When a call is reported, the audio of its last few minutes is kept for our safety team and deleted once the report is closed.

## What happens if rules are broken
Depending on how serious it is: a warning, removal of coins or earnings from the calls involved, suspension, or a permanent ban. Illegal content is reported to the authorities.

If you think we got a decision wrong, write to our [Grievance Officer](grievance).
