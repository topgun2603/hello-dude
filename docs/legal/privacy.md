# Privacy Policy

_Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}_

{{COMPANY_NAME}} ("we") runs the {{APP_NAME}} app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We are the **Data Fiduciary** for this data.

## 1. What we collect

**Everyone**
- **Mobile number** — to sign you in with a one-time code. Other users never see it.
- **Name you choose, gender, avatar, languages** — shown to the people you talk to (name and avatar only).
- **Age confirmation and acceptance of terms** — the date and time you confirmed you are 18+.
- **Calls** — who called whom, when, how long, the rate and the coins charged, ratings and gifts. We **do not record calls**, except as described under Reports below.
- **Coins and payments** — purchases, refunds and every coin movement. Card and UPI details are handled by Google Play or our payment provider; we never see them.
- **Device data** — a notification token so we can ring you for calls and send updates, and sign-in sessions.
- **Chat messages** you send in the app (checked automatically for phone numbers, UPI IDs and payment requests, which are not delivered).
- **Reports and blocks** you make or receive.

**Companions, in addition**
- **Aadhaar Paperless Offline e-KYC** file you upload: we check UIDAI's digital signature and keep your **name, date of birth, gender, photo and the last 4 digits** of the Aadhaar number only. We **never** store the full Aadhaar number.
- **Selfie** taken in the app (to compare with the Aadhaar photo), **PAN** number and card photo (needed for tax deduction), and your **UPI ID** for withdrawals.
- **Earnings, withdrawals and tax (TDS)** records.

All identity documents are **encrypted** and stored privately; only authorised reviewers can open them.

**Safety data**
- If a call is **reported**, the audio of the last few minutes of that call.
- During video calls the app checks the video **on your phone** for nudity. Video is **not** sent to us for this — only if nudity is detected, **one still frame** is sent to our safety team.

## 2. Why we use it
To create and secure your account; to connect calls and bill them correctly; to pay companions and deduct tax; to verify companions are real adults; to keep people safe (reports, blocks, moderation, fraud checks); to send call and account notifications; to answer support requests and complaints; and to meet legal obligations. We do **not** sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with
Only service providers who help us run the app, under contract and only for that purpose: cloud hosting and storage (India), SMS delivery for sign-in codes, Google (Firebase notifications, Google Play purchases), our payment and payout providers, and our voice/video provider. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account profile, languages, devices | Until you delete your account |
| Sign-in sessions | Until they expire or you sign out; expired records are removed after 30 days |
| Call records, coins, payments, earnings, tax records | As long as tax and accounting laws require (up to 8 years), even after account deletion |
| Report recordings (audio) | Deleted when the report is closed (at most 30 days after closing) |
| Flagged video frames | Deleted 30 days after our review |
| Notifications in the app | Deleted 90 days after you read them |
| Chat messages | 1 year after the last message in that chat; messages you sent are deleted when you delete your account |
| Reports and blocks | Kept for safety, even after account deletion |
| Companion KYC files (Aadhaar data, selfie, PAN image) | Deleted when the account is deleted; for rejected applications, 90 days after the decision |
| PAN and name of companions who were paid | As long as tax law requires (for TDS filings) |

## 5. Your rights
You can: see your data and coin history in the app; correct your profile; **delete your account** (Profile → Delete account, or our [account deletion page](delete-account)); withdraw consent (by deleting your account); nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to; and complain to our [Grievance Officer](grievance) and, if not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 6. Security
Data is encrypted in transit; identity documents are encrypted at rest; staff access is limited by role and every admin action is logged. If a breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

## 7. Children
{{APP_NAME}} is for adults only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and delete such accounts when we find them.
