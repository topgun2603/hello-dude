# Delete your {{APP_NAME}} account

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open {{APP_NAME}} and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile → Delete account**.
3. Read what will be removed, type **DELETE** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on every device.

Companions: withdraw your earnings first — the app asks you to before deleting.

## Without the app
Email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** from any address with the subject **"Delete my account"** and your registered mobile number. We will confirm it is you by sending a code to that number and delete the account within **7 days**.

## What is deleted
Your name, phone number, languages, devices and notification tokens, sign-in sessions, favourites, chat messages you sent, notifications, and — for companions — KYC files (Aadhaar data and photo, selfie, PAN card image). **Unused coins are lost** and cannot be refunded.

## What we keep, and why
- **Call, coin, payment, earning and payout records** — tax and accounting laws require us to keep them (up to 8 years). They are no longer linked to your name or number.
- **Reports and blocks** — to keep other users safe.
- **For companions who were paid:** encrypted PAN and name, for tax (TDS) filings.
